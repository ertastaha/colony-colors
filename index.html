Privacy policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Progress and settings stay in the app's local storage. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and may process technical data to deliver and measure ads.

Who we are

Colony Colors is published by Taha Ertaş, who is responsible for the processing described in this policy (data controller). You can reach us at the email address below.

What we collect

The game does not require an account, name, contacts, photos or precise location. Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, fraud prevention and measurement. Non-personalized ads can still use technical data.

What stays on your device

Solved levels, stars, last played level, tutorial state, language, sound, haptics, color symbols, motion, speed and the selected age group are saved on your device. The age-group selection is not sent to a server. We do not operate a server for your progress.

Advertising and your choices

For adults, optional rewarded ads provide one hint without a star penalty after the reward is earned. Occasional interstitial ads appear only between completed levels, after at least three different wins and a two-minute interval. Normal hints and all puzzles remain available offline. We request non-personalized, general-audience ads. No separate analytics SDK is included. Where required, Google's consent form and Ad privacy choices in Settings let you manage your choices.

Security and third parties

Your progress and settings never leave your device. Data used for ads is sent only to Google, our ad provider, over encrypted connections (TLS). Google processes it under its own privacy policy and terms, which provide at least the protection described here. We do not sell data and do not share it with anyone else.

Device backups

If your device backup is turned on (for example iCloud or Android backup), the system may include the game's progress and settings in your own backup. We have no access to it.

Deleting your data

Reset progress in Settings to clear solved levels and stars. Uninstalling removes local app data, subject to system backups. Ad privacy choices, where available, manage Google's consent state separately. Contact us for questions about data handled by the advertising provider.

Legal basis and your rights

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Türkiye), ads use data only with the consent you give in Google’s consent form; you can withdraw it at any time under Ad privacy choices in Settings. Otherwise we rely on our legitimate interest in funding the free game with non-personalized ads. You may ask us by email for access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its use, and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Board, KVKK). Google may process ad data outside Türkiye and the EU, with the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Children's privacy

A neutral age-group screen appears before advertising starts. For anyone under 18, or who does not provide an age group, advertising is disabled and the ad SDK is not initialized by the app. Adults may see general-audience, non-personalized ads after any required consent. No cross-app tracking permission is requested.
